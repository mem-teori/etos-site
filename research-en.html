Research principle

Open evidence — responsible access

Knowledge becomes stronger when test conditions, countercases, negative results and conclusion boundaries can be inspected and challenged.

Openness does not mean releasing operational beta architectures without the constitutional and relational conditions governing their use. M.E.M.I.'s complete runtime, the minimum structure and reconstructable implementation details remain protected.

The boundary

The evidence must be open to inspection. The operational core is not released without the conditions that make responsible use possible.

Evidence status

Shared evidence key

OBSERVED

An outcome is recorded; an explanation is not established.

PLAUSIBLE

Supported, but insufficiently discriminated or replicated.

EARNED

Survived the declared frozen discriminator — only within its scope.

FROZEN

Version, question or test conditions are locked. It does not mean ‘true’.

REJECTED

The frozen test did not pass. The outcome is retained.

NOT CHALLENGED

The test did not reach the claim and therefore cannot count as a pass.

UNDERDETERMINED

The available basis cannot distinguish the outcomes.

HISTORICAL

Retained as provenance, but not current runtime or current evidence status.

M.E.M. · theory and method

The methodological spine of M.E.M.

THREE LAYERS · RECURSIVE

Condition → transition → manifestation

A potential field or necessary conditions must be present; a relation or transition carries a difference; a manifestation becomes observable. The manifestation then changes the conditions of the next course.

RETURNED DIFFERENCE

Reality must be able to answer back

When a manifestation meets what is actually there, a residual Δ appears. If that difference cannot constrain the next state, a system can remain internally coherent while drifting away from reality.

DELAY

Correction acts on a state that has already moved

Registration and integration may be delayed. Drift can accumulate between event, observation and correction. Timing must not be mistaken for mechanism.

STATUS MOVES BOTH WAYS

Promotion, demotion and withdrawal

Observed may become plausible and earned, but a finding may also be suspended, demoted or withdrawn when new discriminators or countercases require it.

Frozen working rules

  • Absence of evidence is not negative evidence.
  • A question may be invalid before a required condition has occurred.
  • Passed is not the same as not challenged.
  • Timing is not by itself a mechanism.
  • Category is not function.
  • Internal coherence is not the same as access to reality.
  • Local success may have moved the burden elsewhere.
  • A negative outcome is a result and must not be rescued post hoc.

Testing the tests

Current test programmes

BETA · NOT A FROZEN BENCHMARK

D_H_T 100 Situation Test v0.2

A/B design using the same local language model alone and through M.E.M.I. S0 → answer → Δ → S1 → revised answer. Pilot 01 produced no functional discrimination: both arms had 20/20 decision_match=false and 0 lookup calls. The pilot therefore tested the language access floor and the test itself first.

FROZEN BEFORE AUTHORITATIVE RUN

Language Carrier Gate 02

Tests LANGUAGE_IN and LANGUAGE_OUT as two stateless one-way lanes. No result claim is allowed before the authoritative run. A pass would speak only to language carriage in the frozen micro-benchmark.

REJECTED · NEGATIVE RESULT

World Model v0.18

All seven technical gates passed, but central substantive gates failed. Frozen classification: NO_CALIBRATED_BOUNDED_READ. The model was not rescued through new thresholds or post-run reinterpretation.

COMPUTATIONAL PROGRAMME · BOUNDED

Examines causal-role carriage and reciprocal necessity through structural transformations. Results apply within the frozen constructed worlds. MCC11 numbers and details with ambiguous provenance are not used as public design claims.
