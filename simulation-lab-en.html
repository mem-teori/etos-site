M.E.M. Research · Simulation Lab · updated 22 August 2026

Controlled worlds
in which models are allowed to fail

Simulation Lab collects frozen, rejected, open and historical simulation series. A simulation shows what happens in the constructed world — not automatically what happens in people, institutions or nature.

⚠️ Results may only be interpreted within the declared model, version and discriminator. Transfer to reality requires a new, independent test stage.

Series 01 · Freedom, path dependence and recovery

HISTORICAL · SIMULATION

The Freedom Metric

A stipulated operational measure of available decision paths. It must not be described as proven ‘genuine freedom’. The historical series reported a decline from 0.82 to 0.19 under high-bias conditions.

HISTORICAL · SIMULATION

Early bias and path dependence

With equal total bias exposure, the early-bias path ended at 0.18, late bias at 0.51 and control at 0.74. The effect may arise from the model's accumulation mechanism and is not a claim about humans.

HISTORICAL · SIMULATION

Metastable low freedom

71% occupied the defined 0.16–0.24 plateau, 14% recovered above 0.5 and 14% ended below 0.08. The plateau is a simulated third outcome, not an empirical clinical finding.

HISTORICAL · SIMULATION

Intervention order

Mild +0.11; corrective +0.18; relational→narrative +0.21. Narrative alone produced 25% collapse versus 5.6% in the hybrid path. The mechanism is stipulated in the model and cannot confirm clinical effect.

PRELIMINARY · NOT FROZEN

Internal state monitoring

The historical preliminary run reported plateau rates of 71% versus 28% and 5/6 activated recovery paths. ‘Meta-awareness’ is replaced with threshold-based state monitoring.

Series 02 · Relational energy and robustness

FROZEN · EARNED IN SIMULATOR

Windfarm Evolution Lab v1.3

Early band_span did not predict later generativity (r = 0.08 / 0.004 / 0.009). Under hard knockout, HIGH versus LOW produced +15.1 percentage points recoverability. Cross-domain generalisation remains plausible, not earned.

FROZEN · CITY ENERGY v0.6f

Sharing and gate affect different measures

Sharing reduced unmet total from 551.02 to 412.67 (about 25%) and import from 10,291.5 to 8,045.4 (about 22%). A 25% gate did not change total damage, but distributed it across 226 versus 198 blackout hours and reduced average size.

OPEN TECHNICAL POINT

Shared maximum-blackout ceiling

All v0.6f scenarios reached 0.063. This may be an implementation ceiling and must not be used as evidence for or against the gate hypothesis until isolated.

NAKSKOV-INSPIRED · v0.3

Biogas reduces modelled oil demand

At 0 MW, oil demand was 8,229 MWh. At 5 MW it fell to 1,702 MWh (about 79%); at 10 MW it reached 0 in the model. Pressure days were 365 in every scenario. The model uses a simplified annual path, not verified operating data from Nakskov's district-heating operator.

Series 03 · Epistemic and agentic behaviour

BETA v0.2 · TEST OF THE TEST

D_H_T 100 Situation Test

The same local language model is tested alone and through M.E.M.I. on missing information, conflicting sources, constraints, replanning, interruption and recoverability. No AGI conclusion is permitted from the pilot.

NEGATIVE PILOT

Pilot 01 did not reach discrimination

Both arms had 20/20 decision_match=false and 0 lookup calls. The M.E.M.I. arm also carried more prompt work. The result pointed to a language-access floor and triggered a new test rather than a rescue interpretation.

FROZEN · OUTCOME OPEN

Language Carrier Gate 02

Tests whether LANGUAGE_IN and LANGUAGE_OUT can be carried as separate one-way roles. The gate speaks only to language carriage. An authoritative user run is pending.

NOT A PUBLIC RESULT

The 1,296-case IDK track

Claude's English draft contained a completed IDK validation while the Danish page did not. The track is therefore not presented as a result until the underlying freeze and result package is identified.

Next discriminators

MATTER · CHEMISTRY

Freeze the test of the Relational Periodic System

Define relational variables, control classifications and blinded chemical targets before the outcome. The track is promoted only if it discriminates or predicts something not already carried by the standard classification.

DOCUMENTATION

Connect artifacts to every result

Version, freeze note, configuration, seeds, raw results, discriminator and claim ceiling must be findable without releasing the protected operational core.

REALITY GATE

Separate simulation from pilot

A positive simulation result does not become empirical through translation. The next stage requires real data, relevant practitioners and pre-declared stop rules.

ENERGY

Test Nakskov assumptions against operating data

Demand profile, production capacity, fuel constraints and residual load must be verified before the scenario can support a local recommendation.

FREEDOM SERIES

Recover and freeze the source lineage

The five historical experiments must be connected to their original code and results. Until then they remain an internal simulation track, not reproduced public evidence.

The simulations are not the conclusion. They are a safe place to discover what we do not yet know.
